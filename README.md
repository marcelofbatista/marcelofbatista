### Hi, I'm Marcelo F Batista 👋

Analytics engineer working with education data in Brazil. Math teacher for 18 years in the public school system, now building the data side of education:

- I created and lead the data work of the **[Inclusive and Full-Time Education Observatory](https://observatorioeducacaoinclusivaeintegral.se.df.gov.br/)** of the Federal District's education department — data warehouse, ETL from the School Census and the school management system, and Power BI dashboards for 800+ schools.
- I founded **[Azup](https://azup.com.br/)**, a gamified learning platform for grades 6–12 with 10,000+ students, where I run the product, the infrastructure and the analytics (acquisition funnels, recurring billing, performance tracking).

#### 🔨 Open-source projects

- **[azupdados](https://github.com/marcelofbatista/azupdados)** — open data warehouse of Brazilian basic education: INEP School Census, IDEB, school-level indicators and INSE as a historical series. Medallion architecture, Kimball model, dbt tests, and enrolment totals reconciled with the official figures (zero difference, 2014–2024). Every data pitfall found becomes a test.
- **[azup-acessibilidade](https://github.com/marcelofbatista/azup-acessibilidade)** — free accessibility widget for WordPress (WCAG 2.2, eMAG, Brazilian Inclusion Law), born from Azup.
